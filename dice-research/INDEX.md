# Dice Dreams album research — INDEX (entry point)
Started 2026-11-03. Read this first every session, then load only the active topic's folder.

**Objective.** Understand how Dice players move through an album, as journeys and sequences of actions: how they start (first album), how they reach completion, and what the end-of-album Trail really asks of them. Find where Dice loses momentum or revenue, and what to keep. Dice is studied on its own terms: methods from the Disney and Domino work are reused, their conclusions and definitions are not.

**Active topic:** Topic 1, first-album experience. Stage: **reopened and answered (Part R)**, top of `topics/t1-first-album/findings.md`. Groups by days left are comparable before entry; time left shapes the first album; album rolls are small next to the roll economy; at equal time since entry all groups play and pay the same, so a more rewarding first album is not shown to build a better player. Recommendation: do not adopt richer first-album payout; the D-4 pack-content test stays optional. Presentation-ready material: `topics/t1-first-album/PRESENTATION_NOTES.md`. Waiting for Eden's review; still on Topic 1.
