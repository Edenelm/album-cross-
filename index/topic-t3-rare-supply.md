# Topic 3: rare supply (Royal / Super / Golden) (closed 10-22; message rebuilt)
**Message (approved):** the difference is the long wait, not the pace. The longest stretch without a new rare is 14% of album time in Dice vs about a third in Domino and Disney (data-story-review §Topic 3).
**Mechanism slide (Eden):** "Dice Always Has a Way to a Super; Domino and Disney Wait for Events". The mechanism comes from the product teams and was not checked in data.
## Key numbers
- Median gap between new rares: 4 / 7 / 7 days (c/rare-timeline-2026-10-20.md). Longest wait: 14% in Dice vs 26–30% in Domino and 28–35% in Disney (c/golden-timeline-2026-10-23.md).
- Goldens arrive steadily: a new one every 2–3 days, with the longest wait 3–6 days. Disney is still behind on Goldens: at close, Disney payers miss 16–18% of them (Dice 6–7%, Domino 10–17%) (c/golden-timeline-2026-10-23.md; c/disney-stalls-missing-2026-10-23.md).
- A Golden could be bought on every one of the last 10 days in all three games (c/golden-offers-by-day-2026-10-23.md). Dice Pricing_CP / Pricing_DDJ packs are not purchases (c/golden-purchase-revenue-2026-10-22.md).
- Domino 20 rares in the last quarter: about half come from rotating events plus the duplicate exchange (23% / 37%). There is no Market source in 19/20 (worktree topic3-reruns; root topic5-rare-sources-2026-10-12.md is old).
- Domino 20 orange "closing tools" on rare-sources-mix split: Q4 = duplicates 37% + paid 4%, Q3 = 8% + 2%; Market = 0 (c/domino-rare-sources-split-2026-10-26.md). Supersedes the "worktree topic3-reruns" line above.
