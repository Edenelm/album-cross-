# Topic 6: after the album (week after, payout, stock, post-album events) (REBUILT 10-24, not closed)
**Current conclusion:** s6a ("start at half") was misleading. Payers who end 4+ away were already at 24–44% of their own pace in the last week before close, and rose to 49–59% in the week after (c/topic6-before-after-2026-10-24.md).
## Key numbers
- Last week → week after (% of own pace), $2–20 a day payers:
  | Album | Finishers | 1–2 short |
  |---|---|---|
  | Dice 63 | 229→110 | 135→90 |
  | Domino 20 | 217→140 | 120→103 |
  | Disney 15 | 188→128 | 150→108 |
  Dice is the lowest for finishers (c/next-session-prompt-2026-10-26.md).
- Prize in days of own play: half of Dice paying finishers get 2+ days, vs 1 in 4 in Domino and 1 in 7 in Disney (c/topic6-finisher-prize-2026-10-24.md).
- Payout after close, with prizes, over 3 albums: Dice and Domino cut about the same (−10% vs −11%) and Disney cuts the most (−16%). "Dice cuts least" is true for album 63 only (c/topic6-payout-3albums-2026-10-24.md).
- Stock at the end of the close day is under 0.1 day of play, in every status (c/topic6-stock-by-status-2026-10-24.md). Free inflow falls about a third after close (c/topic6-free-inflow-oos-2026-10-24.md).
- Over $20 a day: part of the drop is a return to normal, because the normal week is already 93–99% (finishers) and 83–89% (1–2 short) (c/topic6-normal-week-check-2026-10-24.md). The one-week gap is 0.1–0.3% of album revenue (c/a6s9v-gap-pct-2026-10-25.md).
- Dice >$20 finishers drop because they make fewer purchases per buyer, not cheaper ones (c/dice-gt20-finishers-cz-ron-2026-10-25.md).
- Total game revenue in the week after close: the three games are about equal over 2 albums (99–102%) (c/topic6-game-revenue-after-close-2026-10-24.md).
- Post-album events: the spend field counts all purchases made while in the event (c/event-spend-fields-2026-10-24.md). Finished 2+ days early: Dice 63 6.5% of payers, 19.3% of revenue (c/early-finishers-event-2026-10-24.md).
- Domino easy end "pays twice": fits, not proven (c/domino-easy-end-pays-twice-2026-10-24.md).
- Domino 19/20 >$20 1–2 short, week after vs normal week: drop = fewer purchases per buyer (−23% / −16%), not price; still 6.3 of 7 days active; same offers ran. In 20 the slowdown started before close (69% vs 92% of pace). Mirrors the Dice check (c/domino-after-close-20plus-vip-2026-10-26.md; slides a6s8s, a6s10, vipa).
