## Key findings (typical albums 59/61/63)
- **Completion path:** late finishers (144 on close−3..close) = 9–11% of payers, 21–25% of album revenue, 47–53% of final-weekend revenue; 90–96% bought a paid Wild or sticker in the last days (1–2 each). Final weekend 7–16% above payers' own pace (c/dice-completion-path-2026-11-02.md §0).
- **Left behind:** $5+ a day payers 1–3 short at close−4 who did not finish = 7,974 / 5,995 / 8,803 (4.0 / 3.1 / 4.3% of payers, 22 / 16 / 21% of album revenue); only 13–15% bought a Wild or sticker, 56–58% paid for something else that weekend, spend 61–65% of own pace; they miss 2.3 stickers, 2.0–2.3 of them locked. Why they did not buy: not known (price / no offer / gave up).
- **Not finished from 1–3 at close−4** by level ≤2 / 2–5 / 5–20 / >20: 83–88 / 74–78 / 70–74 / 59–62%, stable in all three albums.
- **Potential = scenario only:** final-weekend spend $27–29 vs $118–127 for finishers of the same group; 10% of ≈7,600 behaving like finishers ≈ $70K an album ≈ 0.3% of album revenue (25% ≈ 0.8%). Assumptions in §0ב of the results doc.
- **65 (case):** final weekend 93% of own pace vs 107–116%; early finishers 12% of revenue vs 5–7%; finishers spent $90 vs $118–127. Structure (who is near, who buys) the same.
- **Eden's 5 checks (2026-11-02/03, c/dice-entry-dups-rolls-2026-11-02.md):** first-album players = 19–22% of players, 10–15% of album revenue; new players who start in the first third finish least (6–8% of payers vs 9–11% veterans) and only 28–32% of their payers pay next album (veterans 67–73%). Late joiners get catch-up (new stickers/active day 5–6 → 10–11 new, 20–25 returning); 1,171 last-third joiners finished album 63 in ~7 days ("new kingdom" = 21% of their stickers). Returning from churn = 9–10% of players, 2% of revenue. Duplicates: ~20 stickers/active day all album; from 50 stickers on 77–89% are duplicates (3–5 new/day); 135+ → 96–99%. Finishers' last 5 days (4 before + finish day): x1.5–4.4 own roll pace, 52–70% ran out of rolls; final prize = 15–25% of those rolls for ≤$2 vs 6–14% for >$20 (~1 day of play). Disney/Domino "prize by expected spend" rule = Eden's description, not checked.
- **Where new players stop (11-03, same doc §6):** at every first-7-days spend level new first-third players stop (no activity in the album's last 7 days = left the game) 2–4x more than veterans and ~a week earlier ($0: 63–68% vs 25–32%, day 6–8, ~30 stickers, kingdom 12–13; >$50: 30–48% vs 12–18%). Paying new stoppers ≈10,500/album: 34% stop in week 1 (veterans 13%), 50% at 50–99 stickers (the duplicate zone). No sharp stall unique to them. Hypothesis → test for first-album players only.
- **Early finishers (63):** same pace as late finishers in the same level, no end jump, same or higher album revenue per player → spend moved earlier, not lost.
- Earlier Dice facts (cards t0–t7): finish 10–12% of payers, finishers 21/26% of game revenue; rare waits 14% of album time; far payers' last new rare 3–5 days before close; locked stickers 54–68% via paid Wild; end lift +15..+33% (55–63).
- **Sticker source vs purchase (11-02, album 63):** offer stickers carry the offer name as `sticker_source` = `purchase.source` and the same campaign_id; 94–98% have that purchase ≤10 min before (placebo 0–2%) → purchase-delivered is identifiable. 88.7% of new stickers come from sources never sold (gameplay/events), 3% with any purchase ≤10 min before. Journeys / Dream Pass = paid access, gameplay delivery. Purchase-delivered = 1.4% of new stickers, 1.5% of new Supers, 38% of Wild fills (c/dice-sticker-source-vs-purchase-2026-11-02.md).
