# Topic 4: how the album closes (gate, closing tool) (CLOSED 10-23)
**Message (option A, approved):** "In Dice the end keeps feeding every payer until the last days; in Domino and Disney the far payers stop getting what they need two weeks before close, so they cannot finish" (data-story-review §Topic 4).
## Key numbers
- Far middle payers get their last new Royal 10–13 days before close in Domino and Disney, vs 3–5 days in Dice (all 4 albums). Gap to the gate: Dice 0, Domino about 2/5, Disney about 10/6–7 (c/far-rare-gate-gap-2026-10-23.md).
- 6–11% of payers hold the top gate sticker two days before close. Almost everyone who ends 1–3 short is missing a gate sticker (95–100%) (c/topic4-gates-2026-10-22.md).
- About 1 in 6 payers who reach 1–3 missing go on to finish (16–25%), in all games. The old Disney gap (25% vs 42–51%) came from the cut, not the game (c/topic4-last8-finish-2026-10-22.md).
- Late finishers' last stickers: in Dice, 80–86% are bought; in Domino, 56–76% of finishers used free stickers only (c/topic4-market-split-2026-10-22.md).
- Disney 15 has stalls on days 9–13 and 26–31, when event sources pause; all sets are open by day 7 (c/disney-stalls-missing-2026-10-23.md).
- Burst vs lull: payout 63% vs 53% (c/lull-economy-disney15-2026-10-23.md).
- Domino 20 Movie Twister: 19,125 payers (18% of payers, 31% of revenue), spending ×1.83 their habit, 52% of them above CZ (c/domino-movie-twister-purchases-2026-10-24.md).
- Payers exactly 5 short at close−4 finish 6–9%. Domino 19 had a pile of 19,832 payers at 5 missing, 14% of revenue (c/gate-distance-finish-2026-10-21.md).
- Domino 19 vs 20 daily new stickers (median payer): 19 = 5-day pause days 16–20, median 0 from day 31; 20 = short pauses with jumps, 0 from day 38. Close 138 vs 140 stickers (c/domino-19v20-daily-pauses-2026-10-26.md).
- Domino 20 finishers' last 3 stickers: 81% via Joker from free end events; two end puzzles (JokerPuzzle + SummerFever) (card dx).
- Domino Easy vs Hard: last new Royal 8–12 days before close in both; Hard gets near earlier (19: 40% of Hard revenue near a week out vs 1% Easy) (c/domino-easy-hard-splits-2026-10-26.md).
