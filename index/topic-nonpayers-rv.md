# Non-payers and RV (Domino free finishers)
**Finding:** in Domino 20, 43% of finishers never paid. They took a free RV path: median 2,873 rewarded views, about 90 a day (spec §4 ch4). Ad revenue at risk is $45,084 = 0.28% of game revenue (final-deck-bottom-lines §3). There are 7,517 non-paying finishers (INDEX).
- Heavy RV watching is not new to album 20. In albums 17 and 19 non-paying finishers watched as much or more, and got 22–30 new stickers each from RV (c/domino-rv-3albums-2026-10-24.md).
- Non-payers are 5–15% of finishers in Disney (final-deck-bottom-lines §2).
