# Topic 5 — Conclusions: where new Supers / Royals come from (closed 2026-10-13)

Albums: Dice 61/63, Domino 17/19, Disney 14/15. Population: PU, NOT first album, login ratio (last row) ≥0.5, close day excluded, new rares only (first receipt per user × rare). Detail: topic5-rare-sources-2026-10-12.md (5–5c), topic5-5d-v3-domino-first-royal-gift-2026-10-13.md (5d v4), topic5-5e-other-events-2026-10-13.md (5e). Chart: topic5_rare_sources_by_quarter_v4.png.

## Conclusions
1. Known — In all three games, most new rares come from MTX events. Dice: Trail (recurring MTX) + one-off MTX events = 73–89% of new Supers in Q1–Q3. Domino/Disney: one-off MTX events = 29–76% of new Royals per quarter.
2. Known — The difference is structure, not volume. Dice has one recurring backbone (Trail ≈ half of new Supers, 96% of PU get a Super from it). Domino/Disney deliver Royals in pulses: a different one-off MTX event dominates each quarter (Domino: Challenge, Puzzle, conveyor_belt, PiñataParty, Blast; Disney: Promo Wheel ×4 in album 14, gamified_8box, Sugar Rush, scrolling).
3. Known — Steady free sources are a minority: Daily 3–16%, Momentum 0–24% (Domino/Disney only), Long feature (Disney Magical Friend 10–36%, Domino pixie 0–30%; Dice Pets <1%).
4. Known — Q4 shifts to closing mechanisms in every game: Dice paid Wild offers (11%) + dup exchange (3–12%); Domino dup exchange (23–30%) + Market (album 17: 14%) + paid (10% album 19); Disney Market (album 14: 10%) and Daily (album 15: 16%).
5. Known — Excluding first-album players moves shares ≤2–4pp (Dice, Disney); conclusions hold. First_Royal_Gift is a first-album gift (album 17); in album 19 (Royal Tower launch, 07-06) ~20% of veteran PU also got it.
6. Hypothesis — Rare supply is gated mainly behind MTX events; Dice drips via an always-on recurring event and leaves the end to Wild offers, while Domino/Disney supply comes in event pulses. Not causal.

## For the CRO (one line)
"In all three games most rares come from MTX events; Dice runs them through one recurring Trail, Domino/Disney through rotating one-off events, and every game ends the album with a closing mechanism (Wild offers / duplicate exchange / Market)."

## Suggested test (not a causal claim)
A/B in Dice: move part of the Trail Super supply to a free steady source (or add 1 Super to the Daily calendar) vs control. Primary: PU album ARPDAU (close−1). Guardrails: PU finish rate, Trail participation/revenue, next-album retention.
