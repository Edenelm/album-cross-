# Payout after close with album prizes, 3 albums per game (2026-10-24)

Eden (2026-10-24) approved this run. The question: the claim "Dice cuts the payout after close the least" (a6s7, t6j, t6k) was checked on one album only. Does it hold over the agreed 3-album set?

SQL: `claude/topic6_payout_prizes_3albums_2026-10-24.sql`. It is generated by `claude/topic6_payout_3albums_gen_2026-10-24.py` from `topic6_payout_prizes_by_level_2026-10-24.sql`; only the dates and the album id change.
- Size: about 850 GB in total (Disney 12: 198 GB, Disney 14: 149, Domino 17: 57, Domino 19: 43, Dice 59: 199, Dice 61: 205).
- Raw rows: `claude/results/topic6_payout_3albums_2026-10-24.csv`.
- The latest albums (Dice 63, Domino 20, Disney 15) come from `topic6-payout-prizes-by-level-2026-10-24.md`, where they are rounded to whole %.

## Definitions
These are the same as the latest-album run:
- **Payout:** days 1–3 of the next album vs mid-album (close−18..−5), with album prizes included. It is measured on the whole group (sum won ÷ sum spent on play).
- **Population:** paying players in the standard population, status on close−1.
- **Spend level:** by own pace.
- **Albums:** Dice 59/61/63, Domino 17/19/20, Disney 12/14/15.

## Reading
- **Fact:** "Dice cuts the least" holds in album 63 only. Over 3 albums, Dice and Domino cut about the same (−10% vs −11% for all finishers) and Disney cuts the most (−16%).
- **Fact:** Domino 19 cut less than any Dice album (−6%), and its finishers came back well (134%).
- **Fact:** Disney 14 and 15 cut the same (−14%) but came back differently (135% vs 128%).
- **Fact:** the same holds inside every game: heavier payers have a smaller cut. This is true in all 9 albums.
- **Answer:** the data does not support "a smaller payout cut after close goes with a slower return". The link seen on the latest albums came from one album, Dice 63, whose week after was the weak week of 2026-09-07.

## Recommendation (replaces the TEST in `topic6-payout-prizes-by-level-2026-10-24.md`)
- **Do not adopt, and do not run the test now:** a tighter Dice payout in days 1–3 after close. Over 3 albums the evidence for it is gone. Re-open the test only if a later check shows the link again.
- Slides a6s7, t6j and t6k stay in the appendix as the one-album first pass.
