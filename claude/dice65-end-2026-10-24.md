# Dice 65: what happened at the end (2026-10-24)

Question (Eden): Dice 65 had no end lift (+3%). Were payers too far to reach ≤3 missing before the album's last weekend? Were the locked end stickers free or effortless for finishers? What was the biggest source?
SQL: `claude/dice65_end_2026-10-24.sql` (dice-dreams-dbt; Q1 106 GB, Q2a 16 GB, Q2b 110 GB).
Population: standard-population payers (topic-5 rules). Dice closes on Monday, so close−4 = end of Thursday = the last day before the final weekend.
Tier = album spend per day in the first 75% of the album. Reach = ≤3 missing at close−4.

## Q1: how close were payers before the last weekend?
| Album | Payers | Within 3 at close−4: % of payers | % of revenue so far | Already 144 at close−4 | Median missing at close−4 | 11+ missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 61 | 195,810 | 16% | 39% | 1.1% | 6 | 22% |
| 63 | 203,118 | 30% | 54% | 1.7% | 5 | 17% |
| 65 | 192,616 | 24% | 55% | 2.4% | 6 | 24% |

By spend level (within 3 at close−4 / already 144 at close−4), 63 → 65:
- ≤$2 a day (65% of payers, 14–15% of revenue): 24% → 16% within 3; 11+ missing 21% → 29%.
- $2–5 (16% / 18%): 38% → 35%.
- $5–20 (11% / 37%): 49% → 50%; already done 4.0% → 6.8%.
- >$20 (2% / 31%): 62% → 67%; already done 6.4% → 14.9%.

- **Fact:** in revenue terms Dice 65 was as close as 63: payers within 3 at close−4 held 55% vs 54% of the revenue so far. So "too far" does not explain the flat end.
- **Fact:** the light payers (≤$2) were further away than in 63 (16% vs 24% within 3). They are 14% of revenue, so they cannot explain a flat end alone.
- **Fact:** more of the strong payers had already finished before the last weekend. >$20: 15% vs 6%. $5–20: 7% vs 4%. A finished payer has nothing left to buy for the album in the last 4 days.
- **Hypothesis (not checked):** part of the end spend of the strong payers moved earlier, so they finished sooner. This fits the strongest album days being 10-10..10-12, 8–9 days before close. Next check: daily spend of $5–20 and >$20 payers by days to close, 65 vs 63.

## Q2: the locked end stickers (1 Golden + 2 Supers)
Found from data as the stickers that reach 1% of holders last. The check matches the old slide: 61 = S143 / S120 / G142, 63 = S143 / S132 / G129. For **65 = S142 / S120 / G140**, which reach 1% of holders 3 / 5 / 6 days before close, the same timing as 61 / 63.

Source of the first copy of each locked sticker, paying finishers (144 by the end of the close day):
| Source | 61 | 63 | 65 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid Wild | 54% | 68% | **60%** |
| Bought directly | 20% | 15% | 20% |
| Free Wild: Trail final reward | 15% | 10% | 9% |
| Free Wild: Blast grand prize | 3% | – | 5% |
| Other free (journeys, dup exchange, events) | ~8% | ~7% | ~6% |
| **Finishers who paid for ≥1 locked sticker** | 95% of 19,554 | 99% of 25,204 | **98.5% of 23,210** |

- **Fact:** the locked stickers were not free in Dice 65. 80% were paid, and 98.5% of finishers paid for at least one. This is the same as 61 / 63.
- **Fact:** the biggest source is the paid Wild (60%), bought about 2 days before close. The biggest free source is the Trail final reward (9%).
- Pack keys `Pricing_CP` / `Pricing_DDJ` are counted as free (they are not purchases). The Wild rule `Pricing%` was validated earlier as purchase-linked.

## Reading
- The flat end of Dice 65 is not explained by payers being too far or by free locked stickers.
- The lead is timing: strong payers finished earlier and spent earlier. Not proven; the next check is above.
