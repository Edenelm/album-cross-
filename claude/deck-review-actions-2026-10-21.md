## Eden's eight questions

1. **end-worth** — new title: the end-of-album lift is worth about 2% of revenue. 'Its value is completions' was a claim the deck never measured, so it is now labelled as not measured. Action: judge any end change in an A/B by revenue per player over the album and the next album.
2. **gates** — the % = share of standard-population payers who hold the album's last-released rare (the gate sticker) at the end of 2 days before close. 'Held back' was removed: only Dice confirms the late release is by design. Action: ask Domino / Disney whether the gate is config-locked.
3. **rare-sources-mix** — rebuilt as a 100% bar chart per quarter per game (Dice 63, Domino 20, Disney 15). Title corrected: Dice's Trail gives 38–62% of new Supers in Q1–Q3, not all of them (one-off events give 29–51%).
4. **closing-week** — checked in data (dice-wild-sources-2026-10-21.md): the line that dips is the free Wild line. Free Wilds come from two events: Bonus Wheel 8–7 days out and the Trail final reward in the last 3 days. On days 6–4 no event gives a Wild. Bought Wilds rise every day. This is not a data issue.
5. **half-completions** — 'same start' = the same number of stickers missing 8 days out. It controls for distance only; a matched spend check still shows the gap. Not separated: the late gate Royal 131, no paid Joker before 2–3 days out, nothing new to collect. Action: test 1 with two arms.
6. **staircase** — distance = stickers missing of 144 at the end of the close day. The drop starts before close, so missing the album is not shown to cause it. Action: no compensation after close; act before the last 4 days (tests 1 and 3).
7. **next-album** — moved to the appendix (a method point). Its key fact is in the finishers-hold notes.
8. **jokers** — the two readings are now in plain language, with the decision: keep the two-Joker end; run a one-vs-two Joker A/B if a group worth 2.3% of game revenue is worth testing.
