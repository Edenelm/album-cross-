# Domino 17–20: payers near FU 4 days before close vs end lift (2026-10-28)
Question (Eden): do albums with an end lift (18, 20) have more payers close to FU at the end than 17 and 19?
Population: standard-population payers. Near FU = missing 1–3 set stickers (not jokers) at the end of close−4, not finished. Album revenue = open..close−1.
SQL: claude/domino_near_fu_c4_17_20_2026-10-28.sql (40 GB, domino-dreams-dbt). Album 18 has 168 set stickers (others 144); v1 with a fixed 141+ cut was wrong for 18 and is not used.

| Album | Stickers | Payers | Near FU (1–3 missing) | % of payers | % of album revenue | End lift, last 4 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17 | 144 | 135,879 | 14,950 | 11% | 28% | +4% |
| 18 | 168 | 137,050 | 27,737 | 20% | 37% | +20% |
| 19 | 144 | 119,201 | 5,087 | 4% | 26% | +4% |
| 20 | 144 | 106,762 | 23,465 | 22% | 46% | +11% |

- Fact: the two albums with an end lift (18, 20) had about twice the payers near FU at close−4 (20–22% vs 4–11%) and more album revenue in that group (37–46% vs 26–28%).
- Fit: matches Eden's hypothesis. Not proof: 4 albums only; 18 also had the July 4 sale and a bigger album; 17 (11%) lifted no more than 19 (4%).
- Needs a test to call it causal (e.g., give a random half of far payers extra stickers before close−4 and compare end spend).
