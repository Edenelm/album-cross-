# Album Onboarding Guide 

## 1\. Album \- General Overview (level 35+)

**Source:** [Album \- general overview](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1vyj3Q9wESTWMZuk50z2ZZq6EvzdntaXWX8FutFj-hH0/edit)

The Album is the studio's card-collection meta feature. Players collect stickers (cards), which are automatically placed into their sets, and complete sets and the full album for rewards.

**Why the Album matters:** it's considered the "father of all features" \- the primary, long-term Live Ops anchor for the game. It's built to deliver high-velocity "micro-wins" while acting as monetization glue that ties together all the other game modes. The design leans on a mix of collection, randomness, and strategy \- "one card away" moments, rare cards with limited availability, countdown pressure as the album/season winds down, and personalized offers based on how far along a player's album progress is. It's also highly flexible for Live Ops, which is why so much of this document is about the levers (RSG, Pixie, Bonus Sets, Deluxe, etc.) that get pulled on top of it.

**Card rarities (3 types):**

| Rarity | Notes |
| :---- | :---- |
| Common | Most frequent card type. |
| Golden | Rarer than Common. |
| Royal | Rarest tier. **Not sold directly** \- can only be obtained by choosing it as a joker. Royal cards feed the **Royal Card Tower** mini-game (see section 3). |

**Structure:**

- 12-16 sets per album, depending on planning.  
- Album run length: 42-56 days.  
- Every set always has 12 stickers.  
- Common and Golden cards carry **crowns** indicating rarity within their tier \- from 1 to 5 crowns.  
- Cards are distributed to players via sticker packs, sold/awarded throughout the game.  
- The Album's grand prize is given as coins in allowance: **150** for players in the **Easy** Album Segment, **250** for **Hard**. Each value is calculated as a prediction of that player's allowance at the end of the album (see section 10 for Album Segment).

**How players actually get cards, day to day:**

- Completing mini-games / MTX events.  
- Duplicate-token rewards (the Duplicates Bar, see section 7).  
- Requesting or trading duplicate cards with friends, in-game or via the game's Facebook community group.  
- Purchasing offers.

Stickers get handed out from all kinds of places throughout the game \- but not every pack is worth the same. Some packs are **"vague"** (the player doesn't know in advance what they'll get), and those mainly serve to feed the early album and the Duplicates Bar. The more valuable, guaranteed-value cards mostly come as rewards for completing events, and through purchases.

There's also a social layer around the album: players can send duplicate cards to friends (in-game or via the Facebook group), capped at 5 sends per day, and Golden cards specifically can only be traded during the **Golden Trade Time** event \- not any time. Royal cards aren't tradeable or sellable either, consistent with them being reserved for gameplay/the Royal Card Tower.

The album also has its own small sub-menu (opened from a button inside the album screen) with three destinations: the album's own rules/info page, the duplicates popup, and the **archive** ("My Albums") \- where past, completed albums live after a new one starts.

**Pack & card types:**

1. **Common packs** \- the base, most common pack type.  
2. **Golden packs** \- also sold showing the crown count up front; the higher the crowns shown, the more valuable the pack.  
3. **Royal** \- never sold; only obtainable through gameplay.  
4. **New** offer \- guarantees a new (non-duplicate) card of a specific type.  
5. **Specific** offer \- sells one specific card. Tapping it shows which set it belongs to and how many cards are still missing from that set. A Specific offer always gives a **New** card.  
6. **Bonus Sets** have their own packs / New-card option too (see section 5).  
7. **Pixie** has its own versions too: a Specific Pixie (shows the player's current progress in that set), a Vague Pixie, or a New Pixie (see section 2).  
8. **Joker** \- lets the player pick any card they want, except Pixie or Bonus Set cards.  
9. **XL Packs** \- a booster that makes a pack hold more cards than usual (see section 8\)  
10. **Packs From Levels-** packs can also be found hidden inside regular gameplay levels, not just sold or awarded as events \- see Packs From Levels in section 9\.

## 2\. Pixie Forest \- the Album's "second chase" (level 600+ or Second album)

**Source:** [Album Pixie Forest \- Confluence](https://superplaystudio.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DOM/pages/3512467463/Album+Pixie+Forest)

Pixie Forest is an extra chase we layer on top of the main Album, timed to land toward the **end** of the album's run \- it typically wraps up about **two weeks before the album itself ends**.

The idea behind the timing: by the later stage of an album, a lot of players have slowed down \- they've either completed most of their sets already or lost momentum. Pixie Forest gives them a fresh, short-term goal to chase, and its reward is tied directly back into the album itself: finishing the Pixie chase doubles the reward you get for finishing the album. So the mechanic is designed to re-engage players in the album's home stretch \- get them to complete the Pixie chase, and from there, push them to go finish the album while the payoff is doubled.

How it works, in short:

- One Pixie is tied to each set in the album (so with 14 sets, there are 14 Pixies to find).  
- Pixies come out of card packs \- either regular packs or a special "Pixie" pack \- and are always the last item revealed when a pack is opened.  
- Finding the Pixie for a set doubles that set's reward \- but the player only actually receives the doubled reward once they complete that set. The same logic applies to the grand prize: finding **all** the Pixies doubles the Album's grand prize, but the player only gets it once they complete the Album itself.  
- Everyone gets their first Pixie as a free gift, as part of a short tutorial.  
- A duplicate Pixie isn't wasted \- it feeds into the Duplicates Bar just like a duplicate sticker (see section 7), worth 10 tokens.  
- Running a second Pixie Forest event within the same album is optional, not a given \- it's a call we can make per album. If we do run one, players can either pick up where they left off or start fresh, depending on how that run is set up ("2nd Chance").

## 3\. Royal Card Tower (mini-game) (level 35+)

**Source:** [Royal Card Tower Mini Game \- Confluence](https://superplaystudio.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DOM/pages/3188654331/Royal+Card+Tower+Mini+Game)

- Royal cards are already exclusive/rare by design \- the Tower is another special thing layered on top of that: **every single Royal card the player gets \- new or duplicate \- lets them open one door in the tower.** Nothing is wasted; a duplicate Royal is just as useful here as a new one.  
- Opening a door does one of two things: it either reveals a prize on the spot, or it advances the player up one floor. One door per floor holds a "peon" \- that's the one that moves you up.  
- The goal is to climb through **all** the floors to reach the **grand prize** at the top. Along the way, finishing a floor (tier) also hands out its own prize \- so there are rewards both per-floor and at the very top.  
- Two ways to open the tower itself:  
  1. Automatically, when the player receives a Royal card \- opens straight into the door-picking screen.  
  2. By tapping the Royal Tower badge on the Album screen \- view-only, shows current tier progress, no door-picking.  
- There are two floor types: **regular floors** (always 4 doors) and **bonus floors** (always 1 door) \- the number of doors per floor type is fixed. What's configurable is how many floors of each type there are, and when they show up along the climb.

4\. Ready-Set-Go (RSG) (level 35+)  
 **Source:** [Album Ready Set Go (RSG) Handoff \- Confluence](https://superplaystudio.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DOM/pages/3519840262/Album+Ready+Set+Go+RSG+Handoff)

Ready-Set-Go is a limited-time campaign that multiplies the **coin** reward of a set \- as long as the player completes that set while the campaign is live.

What the player actually sees:

- Sets that are boosted show a multiplier badge (e.g. "x2") right on their set icon in the album grid.  
- While the campaign is running, the Album badge itself shows the biggest multiplier on offer, so players get the hook before they even open the album.  
- When a boosted set is completed, its reward ribbon shows the original coin amount crossed out next to the new, multiplied amount \- so the value bump is visible and satisfying in the moment, not just a bigger number.

## 5\. Bonus Sets (level 1000+)

**Source:** [Album Bonus Sets \- Confluence](https://superplaystudio.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DOM/pages/3151462404/Album+Bonus+Sets)

- 1-3 limited-time, themed sets added during a seasonal album, live only while their MTX campaign is running.  
- Rewards: one reward per completed bonus set, plus a grand prize for completing **all** bonus sets in that event.  
- **Not required** for main Album completion \- bonus set cards do **not** count toward Album progression or the unique-card segmentation functions.  
- Bonus set cards have only **one** sticker type ("bonus") \- no Common/Golden/Royal distinction, and **no crowns** either.  
- Bonus cards can come out of **regular** packs, mixed in with normal cards, or from **dedicated bonus packs**. Just like any other sticker, a bonus card the player doesn't already have is flagged **New** when it's revealed.  
- Multiple bonus-set events can occur during one album, but never back-to-back or overlapping.  
- Duplicate bonus cards feed the Duplicate Bar too, worth 1 token each.  
- Only **New** and **Vague** offers are sold for bonus cards \- there's no **Specific** offer for them (unlike regular Common/Golden cards, see section 1).

## 6\. Album RC Event ("Deluxe Edition") (level 35+)

**Source:** [Album \- Album RC Event (Deluxe) \- Confluence](https://superplaystudio.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DOM/pages/4042260481/Album+-+Album+RC+Event+Deluxe)

- Unlocks only **after a player fully completes** the live album \- converts it into a **Deluxe Edition** of the same album.  
- Unlocking the Deluxe Edition also unlocks a **Deluxe Wheel**. It's fed by the tokens the player earns from the Deluxe progress bar (see below) \- and the last tokens needed to fill it come from the Deluxe grand prize itself.  
- To upgrade a card to its Deluxe form, the player needs to collect duplicates of the regular card.  
- Completing a Deluxe set does **not** give an immediate set reward. Instead, it advances a **progress bar** (fed by an event token given per completed Deluxe set) with tiered rewards, ending in a **Deluxe Grand Prize**.  
- Deluxe cards keep their original tier (Common/Gold/Royal) and visual hierarchy, but get a premium frame/glow treatment and a "D" bookmark \- enhanced, not redesigned.  
- Deluxe cards are **untradable** \- trading one to another player sends them a regular (non-Deluxe) copy instead, with an in-app disclaimer.  
- There's no limit on how many duplicates a player can hold once they're in the Deluxe edition.  
- No RSG, Pixie Forest, or Bonus Sets during the Deluxe edition \- those are all ignored/disabled once a player enters it. If Pixie or Bonus Sets are still live when the player finishes the main album, Deluxe only starts once those side-events are also completed.

## 7\. Duplicates Bar (Duplicates System) (First album: 80+ stickers, or album 2+)

**Source:** [Album Duplicates System \- Confluence](https://superplaystudio.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DOM/pages/2748645410/Album+Duplicates+System)

- Purpose: soften the frustration of getting duplicate stickers in the mid/late album, by giving them value instead of being "wasted."  
- **Loop:** get a duplicate sticker → it converts into tokens → tokens fill a progress bar (PB) → reaching a tier gives a reward.  
- Each sticker type has its own token value regardless of crown count \- configurable per type, including for Bonus and Pixie duplicates. Per the live game: Common \= 1 token, Golden \= 5, Royal \= 25, Pixie \= 10, Bonus \= 1\.  
- Players only ever see the **next** reward, not the full bracket \- e.g. the first reward requires 30 tokens, but how many tokens the *following* reward will need isn't shown until it's reached. This keeps the pacing feeling open-ended rather than like a visible checklist.  
- The reward at each tier isn't random \- it escalates as the player gets closer to finishing the album: early tiers hand out a **New Common**. Once the player has collected all their Commons, tiers shift to handing out a **New Golden**. Once they're down to their last 10 missing Goldens, tiers shift again to a **New Royal**. And once they're down to their last 2 missing Royals, the reward becomes a **Royal Vague**. This escalation is meant to help make sure the Duplicates Bar alone doesn't hand a player their very last missing stickers and complete the album for them.  
- Tiers are grouped into "brackets" that reset periodically, so the bar keeps giving players something to chase throughout the album rather than running dry.  
- Accessible via a **Duplicates** button in the album's side menu, which opens a popup showing the bar, current tier, and reset timer.  
- Works fully offline; recovery is handled via the existing pack-recovery flow (no separate recovery logic needed).


**Worth flagging: this mechanic isn't considered ideal, and there have been attempts to design a better approach \- none have been developed yet.**

## 8\. XL Packs (level 35+)

**Source:** [Album XL Pack Booster \- Confluence](https://superplaystudio.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DOM/pages/3690790913/Album+XL+Pack+Booster)

* XL is a booster that upgrades a pack to hold more cards than usual, with a visual treatment to make it look bigger/more appealing.  
* It's the same pack type underneath, just with extra cards added \- it still shows a crown (Rarity pack) or no crown (Vague pack), depending on the original pack's UI. The tooltip spells out the old vs. new card count.  
* XL doesn't apply to Specific, Joker, or Pixie packs, and no pack \- XL or not \- can ever exceed 12 cards.

## 9\. Packs From Levels (level 350+)

**Source:** [Album Packs \- Level Card Pack (LCP) \- Confluence](https://superplaystudio.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DOM/pages/3661987841/Album+Packs+Level+Card+Pack+LCP)

* Packs can also be found hidden inside regular gameplay levels \- appearing as a face-down tile among the others.  
* Clearing the tiles above it reveals and grants the pack, which the player then opens back at meta once they finish the level (up to 3 packs can appear in one level).  
* If a player is about to fail or quit a level after finding a pack, a warning popup reminds them they'll lose it if they leave.  
* The point of this channel is to give players who don't often finish events or side content \- the "weaker" players \- a steady way to keep progressing on the album just by playing levels. It also nudges players to spend wilds/boosters (loss aversion) and to play higher modes for better packs.

## 10\. Segments & Settings

**Album Segment** \- splits players into **Easy** or **Hard**, based on how much they spent during the *previous* album:

- **Easy** \- spent less than \$300 in the previous album.  
- **Hard** \- spent \$300 or more in the previous album.  
- This is **fixed for the whole album** \- a player's Album Segment doesn't change once the album starts. All new users default into **Easy**.  
- **Owned by Economy** \- they're the ones who update it.

**Super Segment** \- a finer-grained tier layered on top of Album Segment. Unlike Album Segment, it's **not** based on past spend \- it's based on how much the model *predicts* the player will spend by the end of the **current** album. Because it's a live prediction, it can shift as the album goes on, and players can move between Super Segment tiers mid-album. The goal is to monitor money more precisely and let us apply the right treatments/pushes depending on how strong (how much they're predicted to spend) a given user is:

| Tier | Value | Easy (\<\$300 prev. album) | Hard (≥\$300 prev. album) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| High | 3 | \>\$750 | \>\$1750 |
| Mid | 2 | \$250-750 | \$1000-1750 |
| Low | 1 | \$50-250 | \$250-1000 |
| Losers | 0 | \<\$50 | \<\$250 |

*(The Easy/Hard columns above are the player's Album Segment \- it determines which threshold column applies; the predicted-spend value then places them in a High/Mid/Low/Losers tier within that column.)*

- **Owned by the Analyst** \- this is the one that's on you to update. The Super Segment table \- domino-dreams.ba\_prod.album\_super\_segments \- needs to be updated when a new album starts.  
- **Another source to validate at the start of every album:** domino-dreams.bo\_jobs\_prod.smart\_segmantation\_main \- same idea as above, make sure the correct Album ID is set here too.

**BI / economy config file:** [BI config spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1e5Iea9VqelEa5V2A7brH63_kvLfREQP6kegBpNWG5xc/edit?gid=0#gid=0) \- whenever economy updates go out, this needs to be checked to make sure everything in it is still correct.

**Economic plan:** at the start of every album, get the **Album's** economic plan from the economist and update it in the dashboard.

## 12\. Album Timeline & Checklist

A run-through of an album's lifecycle, phase by phase, and what needs to happen at each point.

**1\. Start of album**

* Update the Super Segment and the segments in Smart Segmentation.  
* Update the filters in the Looker tables (see section 11).  
* Get the album's pacing plan from the economist and update the pacing table in the looker tables.  
* After the first run, verify all the Looker tables look correct. If something looks off, check with BI and verify the config file (section 10\) is correct.

**2\. Recurring sync** \- **Sunday & Wednesday**

* **Recurring cadence \- Album Sync:** happens twice a week, **Sunday and Wednesday**. The purpose of the sync is to check pacing/rates, get updated on monetization and that week's events, and propose action items accordingly.

**3\. Q1 (first quarter of the album)**

* RSG usually launches around here. Make sure the album's pacing and the events are set up to match.

**4\. Q2 (second quarter)**

* Bonus Sets usually launch around here. Update the Bonus Sets Looker dashboard, check that users are on pace, and make sure in-game events and offers are lined up with it.

**5\. Q3 (third quarter)**

* Pixie launches. Update the Looker tables, and monitor pacing closely. Over the Pixie weekend \- dig in deeper than usual to confirm users are on pace and progressing the way we want.

**6\. Q4 (final quarter) \- end of album**

* Update the Deluxe Looker dashboard. This is when monitoring should be at its most frequent \- user pacing, treatments, and monetization all need closer attention here.

**7\. Last week of the album**

* Launch all of the album's end-of-run events before heading into the weekend, so there's time to confirm everything's on track with the plan.
